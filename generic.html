Verica Krstevska

A Biotechnology BSc graduate interested in industrial and environmental biotech for the development of sustainable solutions. Eager to use and further develop my knowledge for creating practical answers to environmental problems through approaches to waste valorization, biorefinery, bioproduction, bioremediation and other techniques and innovations for a circular bioeconomy.

This website serves as a collection of experiments and projects that my curiosity and interests have brought me to, and what I’ve learned through following them through. It’s a catalogue of ideas and explorations into topics that resonate with my values and my passion for advancement toward a sustainable society equipped to take care of the environment and nature. The time and effort given to these projects starting from the initial question/fascination, to reading, researching, actively participating, and coming up with solutions that can be created with the resources available at hand, have given me tremendous insight into the complexities of answering and solving those initial questions, which has in turn motivated me to delve deeper, learn more, and continue to pursue this vocation.
