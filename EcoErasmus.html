ERASMUS+ Youth Exchange: “Eco(art), Eco(society), Echo”

Through NGO GRIT as part of the Macedonian team I participated in the youth exchange “Eco(art), Eco(society), Echo”, where we were immersed into different eco practices in the realm of sustainable use of natural resources, lifestyle and art. Here are some of the highlights from the week.

Visiting a beer brewery and the Eco-Museum of Hop-Growing and Brewing of Slovenia

Making beer is a multi-step process which can very shortly be described as steeping barley in water to activate enzymes present in the grains that break down the starch into simpler sugars, which are then used by yeast during the fermentation step to produce alcohol. This step determines the type of beer: ale or lager. Ales are made by top-fermentation, meaning S.cerevisiae rises to the top of the fermenter because of the active fast fermentation process at 15–24°C, giving a fruitier or more complex taste. Lagers, on the other hand, are made by S.pastorianus at a temperature of 4–12°C, this takes a longer time and gives a more neutral and clean flavor – this is called bottom fermentation since the yeast settles at the bottom of the fermenter.

Left: A fermenter in Savinjska brewery named “Deep Purple”. Right: Hops – added to give bitterness and smell, as well as to preserve the beer.

In the Eco-Museum we learned more about the long-standing hop-growing tradition: the process of planting, growing, harvesting and processing of hops, as well as the tools used throughout history. The museum focuses on the relationship between the people of the region and this cherished plant.

Working in a zero-waste garden

In this garden nothing is thrown away, food is eaten, shared, made into juices or fermented into vinegar. The plants are local and the technique of companion planting is used – growing different plants that can benefit each other close to one another, thus avoiding the use of pesticides and herbicides.

Art and nature

We enjoyed the sunny days outside creating art and visiting the nearby lake Vrbje and Pekel cave.
